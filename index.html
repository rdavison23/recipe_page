Misir Wot

Misir Wot — also spelled Misir Wat — is a beloved Ethiopian dish made from red lentils simmered in a richly spiced sauce. It's a staple in Ethiopian cuisine, especially popular among vegetarians and vegans due to its hearty flavor and protein-packed profile.

Ingredients

  • 1 large yellow onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoon ginger, minced
  • 3 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp berbere spice
  • 1 cup red lentils
  • 1/4 tsp cardamom
  • 2.5 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1 tsp salt

Directions

  1. Soak and wash your lentils, then let them soak while you prepare the seasonings.
  2. Heat a large frying pan over medium heat.
  3. Add 2 Tbsp of water and the onion and cook until the onion softens.
  4. Add the garlic and chili powder and continue frying for another minute or two.
  5. Stir in the tomato paste, diced tomatoes, Berbere spice, and cardamom and continue cooking for two minutes.
  6. Add the lentils and broth to the pan. Bring to a boil then turn down the heat and let simmer until the lentils become soft.
  7. Season with salt and pepper and serve hot with Injera flatbread.
