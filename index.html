Misir Wot

Misir Wot — also spelled Misir Wat — is a beloved Ethiopian dish made from red lentils simmered in a richly spiced sauce. It's a staple in Ethiopian cuisine, especially popular among vegetarians and vegans due to its hearty flavor and protein-packed profile.
